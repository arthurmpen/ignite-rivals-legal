Version 1 · October 20, 2026

It is also our data processing policy under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and its regulations, and it contains the information required by the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short: we keep what is needed for your account, your progress and online matches. There are no ads, we use no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data.

## 2. What data we process

- Your account: player ID, friend code, the player name you choose (other players can see it) and the date it was created.
- A device identifier: the game creates your guest account from the identifier that your phone's system gives the game. On our server we only keep a hashed fingerprint of that identifier.
- Your email address, only if you sign in with Google or create an account with an email and password. With Google we receive your Google account ID and your verified email; we do not keep your Google name or photo. If you use a password, we store it as a hashed fingerprint, never as text.
- Your progress: level, experience, coins, gems, items, how you equip your cars, Ignite Pass progress, ranks and statistics (matches, wins, goals, assists, saves, shots, MVPs and demolitions).
- Your online matches: date, mode, stadium, team, result, teammates and opponents of each match.
- Social features: friends, friend requests, parties, invitations and private rooms.
- Your purchases: what you buy in the game with coins or gems and, for real-money purchases, the product, the store (App Store or Google Play) and a fingerprint of the receipt to prevent fraud. We never see or keep your payment details: Apple or Google handle them.
- Your acceptance of the terms and of this policy: the version and the date you accepted them.
- Technical data: your IP address and connection data are used at that moment to connect you and to stop abuse; we do not store them in your account. Match servers keep technical logs of each match (they may include player names and IDs) to fix errors and detect cheating.
- On your phone: the game keeps your settings, your session and a copy of your profile in its own storage.
- Quick chat: the predefined phrases you send during a match reach the players in that match and are not stored.

We do not process your location, contacts, photos or microphone. There is no advertising and no advertising profile. We do not use your data for automated decisions with legal effects on you: skill-based matchmaking is only used to build balanced matches.

## 3. What we use your data for, and the legal basis

- Creating and keeping your account, saving your progress and restoring it on another phone. Basis: the contract you accept when you play (GDPR art. 6.1.b).
- Online matches: skill-based matchmaking, ranks, leaderboards, friends, parties and private rooms. Basis: the contract.
- Purchases: delivering what you buy, verifying receipts and preventing fraud. Basis: the contract and our legal obligations, such as keeping accounting records (art. 6.1.c).
- Security and fair play: detecting cheating, abuse and offensive names, and enforcing the terms. Basis: our legitimate interest in a fair and safe game for everyone (art. 6.1.f).
- Answering your requests and telling you about important changes to the service, the terms or this policy. Basis: the contract and the law.
- Complying with the law and with requests from authorities. Basis: legal obligation.

Authorization (Colombia): by accepting this policy when you enter the game you give us your prior, express and informed authorization to process your data for these purposes, as Law 1581 of 2012 requires. We keep the version and the date you gave it, so we can prove it. You can revoke it at any time by deleting your account or writing to us; without that data we cannot offer you the online features, but you can keep playing offline against bots.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- Your account, progress, friends and statistics: as long as your account exists.
- If you delete your account in the game, we delete it immediately; if you ask us by email, within 15 business days at most. In matches you already played with others, the result is kept and your place shows as "deleted player".
- Real-money purchases: we keep the receipt fingerprint, no longer linked to your account, so it cannot be used twice, and whatever accounting and tax law requires us to keep, locked and used only for that purpose.
- Technical match logs: 90 days at most.
- The game's database remains in force for as long as the game is in service.

## 7. How we protect your data

The connection to our account server is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and sessions are stored as hashed fingerprints. Only the people who maintain the game can access the servers. The real-time match connection is not encrypted, but it only carries game data: your player ID and player name and the moves of the match. No system is completely secure: if an incident affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities when the law requires it.

## 8. Minors

- You must be at least 13 years old to play. We do not seek data from children under 13: if we find out that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it. If you are a parent or legal guardian and think this has happened, write to us.
- In Colombia the age of majority is 18. If you are under 18 (or under the age of majority in your country, if it is higher), you need permission from your parent or legal guardian to play and to buy.
- Authorization to process the data of someone under 18 is given by their legal representative, after hearing the minor's opinion according to their maturity. We only process their data for what this policy explains, respecting their best interest and fundamental rights (Law 1581 of 2012, art. 7, and Decree 1377 of 2013, art. 12).
- In the European Union, if you are under the age your country sets for giving your own digital consent (14 in Spain; between 13 and 16 in other countries), your parent or guardian must authorize it.
- Parents and legal guardians can ask us to see, correct or delete their child's data by writing to our email.

## 10. How to delete your account

- In the game: Settings › Account › Delete account. Once you confirm twice, we immediately delete your account and your data from our server. It cannot be undone: you lose your progress, your items and any gems you have not spent.
- Without the game (for example, if you already uninstalled it): write to [POR COMPLETAR: correo de contacto] with the subject "Delete account" and include your friend code or your account's email. We will confirm the deletion within 15 business days at most.
- Uninstalling the game does not delete your account from the server.
- Afterwards only what section 6 explains remains: the purchase receipt fingerprints, without your account, and whatever the law requires us to keep.
